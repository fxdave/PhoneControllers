Works on the phone with the steering wheel and uses its compass. Center sets straight ahead. More settings are in the PC app, under Look around.

Camera pedal

Cover the back camera with a finger to press the pedal. To calibrate, tap each button while holding the phone the way you drive. With several lenses, pick the one that goes dark in the preview when you cover it.
